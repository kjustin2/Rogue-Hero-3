# UI reference decisions

Keep original geometry and artwork. References guide visual hierarchy and materials.

- Diablo II: an atmospheric setting behind the menu and stone-framed choices.
  https://www.mobygames.com/game/1878/diablo-ii/screenshots/windows/294478/
- Soul Reaver 2: tall gothic lettering and dark ornament.
  https://www.mobygames.com/game/5563/legacy-of-kain-soul-reaver-2/screenshots/
- Blizzard environment notes: grounded materials, controlled light, moving props.
  https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23788294/diablo-iv-quarterly-updatemarch-2022

The owner rejected the resulting frontal cathedral and warm ornate UI. These
references are historical, not permission to replace the accepted combat style.

Current direction: reuse the actual gritty isometric gameplay kit, a fixed
orthographic camera, low burial stones, hard shadows, charcoal and muted ivory.
Early survival-horror influence means negative space and restraint. No frontal
cathedral, oversized flames, bright windows, warm plaques or storybook blackletter.
One locally bundled IM Fell English font serves title, menus, Options and HUD.
Animation is limited to small fires and drifting dust. Saved-location information
is useful; slogans and checkpoint suffixes are not. Inspect Chrome and Electron.
